YT Scheduler

Privacy policy

YT Scheduler is a personal tool operated by Yogesh Kumarasamy. It is a single-owner app. It uploads videos the owner chooses from their Google Drive to their own YouTube channel and schedules when those videos publish.

Google account access

The app signs in with Google OAuth and requests these scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube (youtube)
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload (youtube.upload)
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file (drive.file)
  • openid
  • email

Those scopes are used only to upload the owner's chosen Drive videos to their own YouTube channel and to schedule publishing. openid and email identify the Google account that connected the channel.

Data the app stores

The app stores an encrypted refresh token in Cloudflare D1, and job metadata needed to run an upload (the chosen Drive file, title, description, publish time, and status). The connected account's email address is stored with that channel.

The app does not sell this data and does not share it with other people. It does not show ads.

Retention and deletion

You can revoke this app's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting deletes the stored tokens.

Google API Services User Data Policy

YT Scheduler's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. That use is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
